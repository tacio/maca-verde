# 🍏 Maçã Verde Fitness

> **Daily Progressive HIIT • Text-Neck & Posture Restoration • Belly Fat Burn • Cardio Conditioning**
> Specially tailored for training with only a **Jumping Rope** and a **Fixed Door Pull-up Bar**.
> **100% Local-First Storage with Direct Google Drive Backup.**

---

## 🎯 Core Philosophy & Features

### 1. The "+1% Every Day" Micro-Overload Engine
- **Proven Adaptation:** Large leaps cause injuries and burnout; micro-progressions (+1% to +2% daily) compound into transformational results.
- **Dynamic Daily Challenge:** Each day introduces a personalized challenge:
  - *Door Bar Days:* +2 to +5 seconds on your decompression dead-hang.
  - *Metabolic Days:* Tabata sprints at RPE 8+ to trigger EPOC fat oxidation.
  - *Cardio Days:* +25 to +50 rope turns with posture locked in place.
- **Visual Habit Streak & Levels:** Daily flame tracker with 60-day consistency heatmap.

---

### 2. Tailored Equipment Biomechanics

#### 🚪 Fixed Door Pull-up Bar (Posture & Core Anti-Extension)
- **Spine Decompression Dead Hang:** Opens thoracic vertebrae, reverses cervicothoracic disc compression caused by looking down at phones/laptops ("text neck").
- **Scapular Pull-ups & Retractions:** Slides shoulder blades down and back, strengthening lower trapezius and serratus anterior to permanently eliminate rounded shoulder slump.
- **Hanging Knee-to-Chest / L-Tucks:** Deep transverse abdominis & lower rectus abdominis activation. Fixes anterior pelvic tilt (which pushes the lower belly outward even at low body fat).

#### ⚡ Jumping Rope (Cardio Engine & Visceral Fat Shred)
- **High-Cadence Boxer Skip & Sprints:** 14-20 kcal/min metabolic burn with low joint impact.
- **Postural Cadence:** Trains athletes to keep shoulders back, elbows tucked, and gaze strictly forward at eye level (never down at the feet).
- **Tabata Speed Bursts:** 20s all-out sprint / 10s rest protocols that spike VO2 max and trigger 12-24 hours of Excess Post-Exercise Oxygen Consumption (EPOC).

#### 🧘 Bodyweight Posture Specials
- **Prone Cobra & Cervical Retraction (Chin Tucks):** Fires deep cervical neck flexors (*longus colli/capitis*) while stretching tight pectoralis minor.
- **Prone Y-T-W Scapular Raises:** Isolates rhomboids and mid-traps.
- **Hollow Body Rock to Tuck:** Gymnastics anti-extension core compression that flattens the abdominal wall.

---

### 3. Interactive Athletic HUD & Voice Coach
- **Gigantic Countdown Display:** Clearly visible from 3-4 meters away while skipping rope or hanging from the door bar.
- **Web Audio Synth Beeps:** Real-time 3-2-1 referee ticks and high-energy start/rest chimes (zero external audio dependencies).
- **Speech Synthesis Voice Coach:** Speaks aloud current exercise and **live text-neck cues** (e.g. *"Work! Retract chin and depress shoulder blades"*).
- **Post-Workout Check-in:**
  - Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE 1-10).
  - Text-Neck Discomfort Score (0-10) tracking tension dropping over time.
  - Actual jump rope turns & hang seconds.

---

### 4. 100% Local Storage & Google Drive Backup

- **Zero Cloud Lock-in:** All data resides directly in your browser's local sandbox storage (`localStorage`).
- **Instant JSON Export / Import:** One-click download of `maca-verde-fitness-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`.
- **Direct Google Drive Integration:**
  - Connects directly to your Google account using Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0).
  - Uses the restricted `drive.file` scope (can only touch its own backup file, zero access to your private files).
  - One-click **"Backup Now to Google Drive"** and **"Restore from Google Drive"**.

## 🚀 Running the App Locally

The application is running live:

```bash
# In the repository root
npm run dev
```

- **Local URL:** `http://localhost:5173`
- **LAN URL:** `http://<your-lan-ip>:5173` (Open on your mobile phone on the same Wi-Fi!)

To build a production bundle:
```bash
npm run build
npm run preview
```
